Mastward — Support


Mastward is a sovereign, local-first terminal & AI-agent hub: you drive your own computer from your phone or another device, over your own network. Nothing runs on our servers.


Contact us

Email support@mastward.com. We aim to reply within 2 business days.


Getting started

  • Install Mastward on the computer you want to control (Mac now; Windows is being rebuilt and is not downloadable yet). It self-starts a local engine — no setup.
  • Open the app; on the same machine it connects automatically.
  • To drive it from your phone: tap Connect Phone on the computer, scan the QR with the Mastward mobile app on the same network (or your Tailscale).

    • Common questions

    The terminal shows garbled/overlapping text. Fully close and reopen the app (or hard-reload the web version) to pick up the latest build.

    "Invalid or expired token." Open Settings and re-enter your computer's address and token, or re-pair from the computer.

    A split pane disconnected. Panes reconnect automatically after a moment; if not, close and re-add the pane — your session survives on the host.

    "This phone" Python. An optional offline Python sandbox that runs inside the app. On iPhone it uses the bundled standard library only (no internet package installs), for App Store policy.

    Where are my keys / data? On your own machine, encrypted locally. We never receive them. See our Privacy Policy.


    Pro / licence

    Mastward is buy-once (no subscription). Enter your licence key in Devices → paste licence. Licences validate offline and never phone home.
